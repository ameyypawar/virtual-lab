Computer Networks Lab

The Computer Networks Lab is designed to simplify complex networking concepts through interactive simulations. From CSMA/CD to DNS, this lab enables students to visualize, analyze, and optimize network protocols and data flow with hands-on precision. Students will explore protocol design, understand data flow, and analyze network performance.

This virtual lab provides a comprehensive environment for students to experiment with various networking concepts in a safe and controlled setting. Through guided experiments, students gain practical experience that complements their theoretical knowledge of computer networks.
