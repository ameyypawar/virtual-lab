Target Audience

This lab is intended for the following audience:

  • UG students — B.E./B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering, Information Technology, and related disciplines studying Computer Networks.
  • PG students — M.E./M.Tech students specializing in Computer Networks, Cyber Security, or Information Security.
  • Faculty members — Educators who wish to use interactive simulations to supplement their classroom teaching of computer networking concepts.
  • Researchers — Individuals exploring network security, protocol analysis, and penetration testing methodologies.
